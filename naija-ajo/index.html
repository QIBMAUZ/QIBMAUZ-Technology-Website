Compliance note

Transparent coordination, not financial returns.

Naija Ajo is a contribution group coordination and record-keeping platform. It is not an investment platform, lending platform, wealth management service, or provider of guaranteed financial returns. Participation in any contribution group remains subject to the agreements and trust relationship among group members.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Naija Ajo?

Naija Ajo is a digital platform that helps trusted groups coordinate contribution activities and maintain transparent records.

Does Naija Ajo guarantee returns?

No. Naija Ajo does not provide investment services or guarantee returns.

Who manages the groups?

Group administrators create and manage their own groups.

Does Naija Ajo hold funds?

Payments are processed through licensed payment providers. Naija Ajo coordinates records, contribution tracking, and payout workflows.
